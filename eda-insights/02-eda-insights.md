# V2 insights Charles River

Version 2 of the insights for Charles River EDA

## Macro Analysis: The State of the Company

### 1. Baseline Trends

- **Revenue:** Stable with a moderate upward trend year-over-year
  (~$2.2M to ~$2.6M monthly).
- **Profitability Benchmark:** Gross Margin remains resilient, fluctuating
  around a baseline of ~47.2%.

### 2. Identified Anomalies & Cyclical Dips

- **Autumn Margin Dips:** A noticeable recurring drop below 47% occurs between
  September and November in both 2025 and 2026.
- **Other Outliers:** Transient dips occurred in April 2024 and the mid-year
  window of May–August 2026.

### 3. Hypotheses for Further Investigation (Next Steps)

- _Product Mix Effect:_ Did sales shift toward lower-margin goods
  (e.g., Furniture at ~43%) away from high-margin accessories/services?
- _Commercial Pricing/Discounts:_ Were seasonal or volume discounts granted
  ahead of year-end?
- _Customer Concentration:_ Did large wholesale/strategic accounts dominate
  order volume in those specific months?

## Product group profitability comparison

- Furniture brings in the most revenue (~$50M), but has got the smallest profit
  margin (~43%)
- Accessories generate small revenue (~5.7M), but have got the biggest
  profit margin (~50.5%)
- Services generate 100% profit margin on materials.

## Customer segment profitability and growth levers

- **Strategic clients** are second smallest group, yet they bring in the second
  largest revenue
  ($26M), however their margins are just at ~43.1%.
- **Design Trade** and **Small Business** bring in the best margins
  (apart from design services.) Getting 10 more clients from this segment will
  bring in much cleaner revenue
  than **strategic clients**.
- **Design Services** generate the most clean revenue (100% profit margin).
  They should be marketed more, as they're much more profitable to the company
  in the long run.

## Physical order size tiers, product group mix and customer segmentation

- **Furniture** is the most often sold category
- **Large** orders (21-50 units) are the biggest group.
  The average price of one unit is also the highest in this group
- The company sells the most and the biggest orders to the
  **wholesale** segment

## Product-level profitability

- Brookside Bench generates high revenue ($1.24M), but the margin is just 33%,
  making the gross revenue only $411K.
- Willow Desk generates $1.88M revenue, and it's margin is at 48.3%, making it more profitable to sell.
- Textiles and Lighting achieve really high margins (above 50% - the highest in the top 15
  most profitable products)
